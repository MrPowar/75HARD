The Rules

75 Hard is not a fitness challenge. It is a transformative mental toughness program. If you fail to complete any daily task, your progress drops to zero and you start from Day 1. There are no excuses.

1

Follow a strict diet with absolutely no alcohol and NO cheat meals.

2

Complete two 45-minute daily workouts. One MUST be outdoors.

3

Drink 1 US Gallon (3.8 liters) of water.

4

Read 10 pages of a non-fiction entrepreneurship or personal development book.

5

Take a daily progress photo.

6

Mandatory non-negotiable sleep (At least 6 hours, configured by this tracker).
